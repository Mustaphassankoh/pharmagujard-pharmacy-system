About

Designed to make pharmacy work more structured and explainable

PharmaGuard is an academic software project that brings operational pharmacy workflows and explainable clinical review into one coherent application.

Operational clarity

The platform connects medicine records, batch inventory, stock movements, dispensing, and transaction history so staff can work from consistent information.

Responsible decision support

Clinical checks remain deterministic and reviewable. The AI prototype only prioritizes review and never replaces pharmacist judgement.

Academic prototype

PharmaGuard is developed for demonstration and evaluation. It is not a clinically validated medical device and should not be treated as one.

How it works

A clear path from setup to governed dispensing

PharmaGuard organizes pharmacy work into traceable stages.

  1. 01

    Create the pharmacy workspace

    Registration creates the pharmacy and its first administrator together.

  2. 02

    Configure medicines and inventory

    Add categories, medicines, and dated stock batches with pricing and quantity information.

  3. 03

    Start a dispensing workflow

    Choose Direct Sale, External Prescription, or Consultation and add medicines.

  4. 04

    Run the clinical review

    Review applicable drug-interaction, allergy, and dosage results alongside advisory AI priority.

  5. 05

    Confirm and retain evidence

    Acknowledge warnings where required, confirm dispensing, allocate stock through FEFO, and retain the transaction history.
